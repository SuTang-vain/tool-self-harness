# Adversarial Pre-Submission Review — "Harnessing the Harness"

**Reviewed artifact:** `experiments/tool-side-harness/PAPER_DRAFT_V1.md` (v1, 2026-08-16)
**Cross-checked against:** `RESEARCH_CHARTER.md`, `EXPERIMENT_ARCHIVE_V1.md`, `INTEGRATION_MEMO_CORDIS.md`
**Reviewer stance:** Independent, adversarial but fair. NeurIPS-workshop scale (Soundness / Presentation / Novelty / Contribution-significance, each /5).

---

## 1. Summary

The paper reports a self-study in which the DeepSeek Harness (DSH) — the very harness hosting the research — is itself treated as a tool-side harness target, run through the program's frozen qualification/evolution pipeline. It claims (i) two DSH targets reach formal baselines with discriminating gradients on all four partitions; (ii) a bounded −44% realm-rule rewrite recovers a degraded capability and replicates locally (E2); (iii) efficiency effects are below measurement resolution; (iv) a "prior-distance" mechanism — causal effect size scales with distance between harness content and model priors — is directly measured by a no-harness guessability battery (G) calibrated against effect sizes, and is "usable as a pre-pilot gate"; and (v) the live runtime's revertibility was measured (M3/M5/M6), "turning the Cordis paper's revertibility claim into a measured behavioral surface." The strongest stated claims are E2 and E3-directional, and the paper repeatedly asserts "nothing is overstated."

## 2. Strengths

1. **Preregistration and negative-result discipline are genuinely exemplary.** The efficiency promotion was retracted after a −0.1% replication (§3.3), the cross-repository non-transfer (R3b) is reported rather than hidden (charter item 11), and the failed forward validation of the gate is treated as a first-class result (§4.3: "each failure upgraded the measurement protocol rather than being hidden"). This is rare honesty in self-evolution work.
2. **The prior-distance / guessability (G) idea is original and cheap.** Using no-harness single-shot probes scored with grader-equivalent regexes to predict intervention effect size (§4.2, §6 last paragraph) is a concrete, useful screening tool the field lacks; the authors correctly note they found no published counterpart.
3. **Runner-integrity-as-protocol-property is a real methodological contribution.** The escape audit (38 confirmed accesses + one `$HOME`-assembly evasion) and the resulting runner hardening (§2.2) ground a defensible, preregistered stance against the 2026 benchmark-integrity wave rather than a post-hoc one.
4. **The boundary catalogue B1–B5 is specific and falsifiable.** Multi-item dilution, exact-phrase paraphrase, context-reintroduced priors, reasoning-channel/reasoning-floor artifacts, and de-naming each identify a concrete, testable failure mode — a durable negative-result contribution even if the positive mechanism claim is weak.
5. **The audit trail is mostly checkable.** Nearly every headline number is keyed to a frozen record, and §5's claim-structure paragraph is a model of the narrowest-supported-level discipline the charter demands.

## 3. Weaknesses (ordered by severity)

**W1 — Internal numeric contradictions and omitted/stale results (trust-fatal).** A reviewer must be able to trust the numbers, and here they do not reconcile:
- §3.1 reports headless **held-out no-harness = 0/6**, but §3.5 reports the GLM headless held-out gradient as "6/0/1" — i.e., no-harness **1/6**. The charter (item 8: "held-out 6/6 → 0/6 → 1/6") and the archive index (`headless 9/3/3+6/0/1`) both say **1/6**. The results table contradicts the paper's own §3.5.
- Charter item 8 gives headless **held-in official-full = 8/9**, while §3.1 and the archive index give **9/9**. One of these is stale; the paper does not flag it.
- **M5 status is contradictory across documents.** The paper describes only a 3-cycle pilot ("three cycles … 27 lifecycle operations", §5.6) and lists "the M5 eight-cycle full run with idle controls (A1)" as *future work* (§7). The integration memo's M5 row, however, records "**pilot + Full done** … Full 8 cycles C4–C11 with A1 idle controls: H5.1 8/8, H5.2 0 errors (107 ops cumulative)". The archive index §8 still lists "M5 Full (8 周期 + A1 空闲对照)" as a pending entry condition. Three documents, three states; the paper and memo cannot both be right.
- **The paper omits its own stronger Q3 result.** §3.3 concludes "Q3 effects require variance-controlled designs" and §7 lists "Q3 variance-controlled designs" as future work — but charter item 16 records that the variance-controlled design **already ran** (n=25/arm, paired, −5.9%, |Δ|=0.36 SD, below the 1.5-SD bar) and that its conclusion is "a *different frame* (larger suite, more attempts, or a different metric), **not more repeats of this design**." The paper's stated remedy is exactly what the charter says failed.

**W2 — The headline mechanism claim ("predictor usable as a pre-pilot gate") is overclaimed against its own prospective record.** Conclusion (d) asserts prior distance is "a measured, calibrated, model-specific predictor usable as a pre-pilot gate"; the abstract and title ("…That Predicts It") repeat this. But the gate's only forward validation **failed**: 4/4 anchored predictions out-of-range, improving to 1/4 in v2 (§4.3). "Calibrated" rests on 6/8 (GLM, n=8) and 4/4 (DeepSeek, n=4) pairs — see W4 — with two of the eight exceptions explained *post hoc* (B1, B2). Naming an exception is not calibrating it. §5's own "E1-methodological" label for the mechanism is correct; the abstract/Conclusion language is not.

**W3 — "E2" labels are misapplied to runtime infrastructure, and the abstract overstates M3.** §5.6 labels M3 and M5 "(E2)", and the abstract states M3 "reversed all four host effect classes with zero registry residue." Under the charter, E2 = "Gate 1 passes on one model and one frozen suite" — a Q1/Q2 outcome on a 4D vector, which registry cleanup is not. Moreover, the §5.6 body admits only the **tool** was directly witnessed (36→35); listener/timer reversal is "mechanism-inferred," the provided service's reversal is never accounted for at all, and the client Slot was deferred. "Reversed all four" is therefore an overstatement of a single directly-observed count change. This elevates a runtime-instrumentation observation to parity with the h1 capability claim — a category error in the charter's own terms.

**W4 — Statistical rigor is absent where it matters most.** n=3 fresh repeats underlies every baseline/capability number; there are no confidence intervals, effect sizes, or standard errors anywhere. The calibration's 6/8 is chance-consistent under a sign test (two-sided binomial p≈0.29) and 4/4 is p≈0.125; neither approaches a defensible "calibration." "Replicates" is used for a single second run (§3.2 "replicated locally," seed 20260820) and a single cross-model run (§3.5, four partitions). The paper is honest in §5 ("No significance claims are made at 3-repeat granularity"), but the abstract and Conclusion undercut that honesty, and "n = 8–12 calibration pairs" (§5 threats) is imprecise: it is 8 GLM + 4 DeepSeek, not a per-calibration 8–12.

**W5 — The self-study conflict of interest is only partially disclosed.** §1 discloses the Cordis topical overlap and says the mapping is "not an endorsement." But it does not disclose the deeper structure: a single operator is simultaneously subject, evaluator, and (via the Cordis paper) theorist of its own runtime. The integration memo's stated purpose is that this paper and the Cordis paper are **mutually citing** ("citable from both … so that (a) the Cordis paper can cite the program as prospective validation evidence"), with overlapping authors (Peking University & DeepSeek-AI). The §1 sentence does not surface this mutual-citation incentive; "single operator" appears only as a data-collection threat in §5.

**W6 — Mainline coherence is diluted by four side-threads.** The prior-distance mechanism (§4) is the title's through-line but occupies roughly a sixth of the paper, while §2.2+§6 (benchmark integrity), §5.5 (Cordis correspondence), §5.6 (runtime layer M3/M5/M6), and §6 (identifier semantics) each pull in a different direction. The runtime layer in particular has **no prior-distance content** — it measures the Cordis runtime's cleanup, not model priors — and reads as a second paper. The abstract's attempt to unify everything under "harnessing the harness" does not survive the mechanism claim's dilution.

**W7 — §5.5/§5.6 edge toward validation overclaiming of the Cordis calculus despite the honesty clause.** The honesty clause ("descriptive, not a validation of the calculus") is good, but §5.6 immediately asserts "The temporal side of §5.5 is no longer only correspondence" and "Together M3–M6 **turn the Cordis paper's revertibility claim into a measured behavioral surface**." A single-plugin probe plus a 3-cycle registry check does not "turn a revertibility claim into a measured surface"; the memo's own honesty clause says "none of the program's results should be cited as validation of the calculus itself." The paper leans past that line in tone.

**W8 — Related-work demarcation is thin and partly rhetorical.** All citations are within ~two months of the draft (arXiv:2602.16666 through 2608.05810), so currency is fine but demarcation is shallow. The Double Ratchet contrast ("opposite design choice: graders frozen and audited") is undercut by the admission that the program *hit exactly their gaming trap once* ("our checker-feedback trap (pilot v2) is an instance of their gaming warning"). §2.2's "our position differs in kind, not just in time" is rhetorical given the hardening **changed a headline result** (headless held-out minimal 6/6→0/6, charter item 10) — some numbers were in fact corrected by the enforcement the paper claims preceded them.

## 4. Questions to Authors

1. Which is the correct GLM headless **held-out no-harness** score — 0/6 (§3.1) or 1/6 (§3.5, "6/0/1"; charter item 8; archive index)? And why does charter item 8 report headless held-in official-full as 8/9 while §3.1 and the archive say 9/9?
2. Is the M5 **eight-cycle full run done** (memo: H5.1 8/8, 107 ops) or is it *future work* (§7)? Please reconcile the paper, the memo, and archive §8, and state which frozen record is authoritative.
3. Why do §3.3 and §7 present "variance-controlled designs" as the future remedy when charter item 16 already ran the n=25/arm paired design (−5.9%, |Δ|=0.36 SD) and concluded a **different frame** is required? Will you report the n=25/arm result in the body?
4. Given the gate's prospective record is 0/4 then 1/4, what evidence supports "predictor usable as a pre-pilot gate" (Conclusion d)? What is the preregistered success threshold that the gate must meet before this language is justified?
5. Under the charter, E2 requires a Gate-1 pass on a 4D outcome and a frozen suite. How do M3 (one tool-count change, n=1, two classes mechanism-inferred) and M5 (registry dimensions, no Q1/Q2 outcome) satisfy E2, as opposed to E1-methodological?
6. The abstract claims M3 "reversed all four host effect classes," but §5.6 directly witnesses only the tool and never states the provided service's reversal. What was directly measured — versus inferred — for the service, listener, and timer?
7. What is the preregistered statistical plan for the calibration (effect-size threshold, confidence intervals, multiplicity control)? 6/8 and 4/4 are both consistent with chance under a sign test; how is "calibrated" justified?
8. The memo states this paper and the Cordis paper are to be mutually cited as each other's validation evidence, with overlapping authors. Does §1's disclosure capture this mutual-citation incentive and the single-operator self-study structure, or only the topical overlap?

## 5. Scores

- **Soundness — 2/5.** The headline mechanism rests on n=8/n=4 calibration pairs (both chance-consistent, no CIs, post-hoc exceptions) and a failed (0/4→1/4) forward validation, while internal numeric contradictions (headless held-out; M5 status; omitted n=25/arm Q3 result) undermine trust in the reported numbers.
- **Presentation — 3/5.** Clear scaffolding and honest boundary sections, but four side-threads dilute the promised through-line and the abstract/title oversell what the body actually measured.
- **Novelty — 3/5.** The guessability-gate measurement idea is genuinely new and useful; the frozen-grader discipline and runtime-reversibility material are incremental or belong to the Cordis paper.
- **Contribution-significance — 2/5.** The preregistered negatives and the B1–B5 boundary catalogue are valuable to the community, but the central positive claim (a usable predictor) is not yet established and the runtime material is prospective-application evidence at best.

## 6. Verdict

**Borderline (leaning Reject in present form) for a workshop.**

**Single most important fix:** Reconcile the claim language and the numbers with the frozen archive before submission — correct the §3.1 headless held-out cell (0/6 vs §3.5's 1/6), state the true M5 full-run status, report the n=25/arm Q3 variance-control result instead of citing variance control as future work, downgrade the abstract's "reversed all four host effect classes" and Conclusion's "predictor usable as a pre-pilot gate" to match the body (one directly-witnessed reversal; 0/4→1/4 prospective record; recall-dominant content only), and re-label M3/M5 as E1-methodological. A workshop paper lives or dies on whether its frozen records are internally consistent and its claim levels are honest; today both are in question despite the paper's own stated intentions.
